We are the ACCB

The Augusta Curtis Concert Band is a community band filled with members ranging in age from early teens through their golden years. The band rehearses every Monday from 7:00 to 9:00 pm in the lower level of the Augusta Curtis Cultural Center. Anyone interested in playing with the band is welcome to join the fun on Monday evenings!
